Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Play the story (chapters 1–4, then the finale). Story and mission trophies unlock as you go — 19 of them, including the gold for the final mission. Do the meditation spots (#28) as you explore: it is the one missable trophy.
  2. Clear the contacts and strangers. Finish every contact's last mission (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and all 8 stranger strands. These unlock between chapters, so sweep the map again after each chapter.
  3. Activities and collectibles. Shooting range golds, drone races, card sets, WAiNK spots, stunts and challenges — the checklist below tracks all of them, and the filters isolate each kind.
  4. Combat, money and exploration. Bank vaults, armored trucks, vehicle discovery, garage properties and the 52 areas of Leonida. Then the 100% completion gold, and the platinum is yours.

One trophy is missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots before the story moves past them. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the finale.
