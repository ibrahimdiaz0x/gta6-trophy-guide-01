Read this before Chapter 1 ends

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy the published guides flag as possibly missable. The first meditation — at Jason’s house, after the mission Down to Earth — is only offered in Chapter 1: talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. PowerPyx notes it is still unconfirmed whether that spot is required for the trophy, so do it anyway. Everything else in this list can be earned after the story, in one playthrough, with no online play.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions. PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and puts the platinum near 200 hours; Push Square warns to set aside up to 300 hours. Most of that time is the 100% checklist in Stage 6.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, and meditate in Chapter 1. Finish Chapters 1–4 and the final mission (12 story trophies). Do the Jason’s-house meditation before Chapter 1 ends. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off.
  2. Stage 2 — Side characters. Finish the final mission for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and complete 8 Strangers & Freaks strands. GTA Zone reports Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count toward the 8; Andy, Billy, Tyrone and Yasmani have their own trophies and do not.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win a drone race at all 14 Top Fun locations, win 20 different races, land 250 stunts, beat Cal at fishing, finish one arcade game and one bowling game, and meditate at all 18 spots.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles and the map. Complete 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, and clear the fog from all 52 areas of Leonida.
  5. Stage 5 — Crime and money. Rob 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs), 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, and an armored truck; launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy the 8 garages, and register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app.
  6. Stage 6 — 100% Completion. Open the checklist in the Progress menu and clear every line. Only 15 of the 18 meditations are needed for 100%, but all 18 are needed for Positive Mental Attitude.
  7. Stage 7 — Platinum. Vice Got a Price! pops the moment the other 54 are done.
