Roadmap to the platinum

Four phases, in order. The platinum is the reward for finishing all of them — there is no shortcut trophy, and only one trophy in the whole list can be missed.

  1. Finish the story. Chapters 1–4 and the finale give you 19 trophies, including gold for the final mission. While you play, meditate at every spot you find (#28) — it is the only missable one.
  2. Wrap up contacts and strangers. Every contact's final mission (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) plus all 8 stranger strands. New strands appear between chapters, so re-sweep the map after each one.
  3. Activities and collectibles. Golds at the shooting range, drone race wins, card sets, WAiNK spots, stunts and challenges. The filters below isolate each kind so you can clear them one at a time.
  4. Combat, money and exploration. Vaults, armored trucks, 100 vehicles, garage properties and all 52 areas of Leonida. Close with the 100% completion gold — the platinum pops with it.

Watch out for Positive Mental Attitude: meditate at all 18 spots before the story moves past them. Every other trophy can be finished in free roam after the finale.
