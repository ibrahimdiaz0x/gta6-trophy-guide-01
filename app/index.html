How to reach the platinum

  1. Play the story first — chapters 1–4, then the finale. The story and mission trophies unlock as you go, 19 of them, including the gold for the last mission. While you explore, do the meditation spots (#28): that is the one trophy you can miss.
  2. Finish the contacts and the strangers. Clear every contact's final mission (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and all 8 stranger strands. They open up between chapters, so sweep the map again after each one.
  3. Work the activities and collectibles. Shooting range golds, drone races, card sets, WAiNK spots, stunts and challenges — the list below tracks them all, and the filters pull each kind out on its own.
  4. Combat, money and exploring last. Bank vaults, armored trucks, discovering vehicles, garage properties and all 52 areas of Leonida. Then the 100% completion gold, and the platinum follows.

Only one trophy is missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots before the story moves past them. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the finale.
