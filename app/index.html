⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation is Lucia's on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, and both GTABase and PowerPyx flag it as possibly missable: it is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. Whether that spot is required is unconfirmed, so do it while Chapter 1 is still open. Everything else is reachable in free-roam after the story.

Sources: PowerPyx · EGamers

Good to know

  • One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay, but nothing is locked after the story — free-roam stays open.
  • Cheats switch trophies off. GTABase and TechWiser report that any cheat disables trophy tracking and saving; the only fix is a clean save.
  • 100% is lenient. PowerPyx puts it at roughly 60% of true completion; you need 70 of the 80 Challenges. Only Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races and Bret's Quests ask for everything.
  • Five trophies are still unconfirmed. In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed method yet (18 Nov 2026). Their cards say so plainly instead of guessing.
  • Progress lives in your browser. Tick a trophy and it is saved locally. Export to back it up, import to restore it on another device.
